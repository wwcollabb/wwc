Frequently Asked

Answers to common questions

How quickly are deposits credited?

Deposits are credited to your wallet within 5–10 minutes after the payment is verified and approved by our team. You'll receive the full amount in your available balance.

How long do withdrawals take?

Withdrawals are typically processed within 30–60 minutes. Once approved, the amount is credited to your registered UPI ID or bank account.

Is my money safe with WinKom?

Yes. We use 256-bit SSL encryption, multi-factor authentication, and 24/7 monitoring to protect every transaction and every piece of your personal data.

How does the referral program work?

Share your unique referral link with friends. When they sign up and make a qualifying deposit, you earn ₹39 and they get ₹21 as a welcome bonus.

Can I withdraw at any time?

Yes. You can withdraw your available balance anytime, subject to the minimum withdrawal limit of ₹100. Investments themselves unlock their returns at maturity as per the chosen plan.
